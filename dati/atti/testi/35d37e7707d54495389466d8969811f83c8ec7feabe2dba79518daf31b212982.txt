 You are here: Home 1 / Tools and customer care 2 / Sensus access Converting files into alternative and accessible formats Sensus Access 
 
 The service allows you to convert your readings, slides, web pages and documents in general into the most suitable formats for your needs: 
 
 accessibility. Otherwise inaccessible documents (e.g. image files JPG or image-only PDFs) can be converted into files with more accessible formats, including tagged PDF, DOC, DOCX, Word XML, XLS, XLSX, CVS, text, RTF and HTML 
 audio (MP3 files) 
 e-books 
 Braille format 
 Audiobooks structured in DAISY format 
 
 SensusAccess is particularly useful if you have difficulty with printed text , want to experiment with different learning styles, need assistance when reading text in a foreign language, or simply want to make your readings available on the go. 
 
 Recipients 
 All university users with an @polimi.it or @mail.polimi.it email address

 Software support Access the service 
 
 Go to Converting files into alternative formats 
 The service is also available on the Online Service portal 
 How does it work 
 
 Upload the document 
 Select the format you want the document to be converted to 
 Enter your email address (@polimi.it or @mail.polimi.it). 
 When your document is ready (after it has been converted), an email will be sent to your inbox. 
 Download the document from the email. The processed file will then be available for 7 days. After this deadline, the file will automatically be deleted from the server. 
 
  The quality of the converted file depends on the quality of the original file. See the information material to learn more about how the service works:
