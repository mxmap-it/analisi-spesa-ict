 instacount® instrument management
 
 
 
 
 

 
 


 
 
 




 
 

 
 

 
 
 


 
 

 
 

 
 Why is B. Braun the partner of choice? 
 More than 155 years of expertise in the field of Instrument Management More than 1000 collaborative projects internationally performed since 2008 Active instrument management consulting in more than 40 countries Innovative product and service portfolio in the field of Instrument Management Vast experience in planning, managing and operating own in-house and external CSSDs in several countries

 Implementation of products, software and processes 
 We can support you to seamlessly integrate your new products, empower your CSSD and OR teams with digital tools, and implement operational processes so you can focus on your core business.​ For example: Our experts will implement your brand new surgical assets into your reprocessing cycle.​ 
 We help you integrate digital tools into your daily operations, such as integrating your backup stock inventory management data into the Backup Stock Management App.​ 

 
 


 

 

 
 
 

 

 
 
 

 

 
 Back to Top 
 

 
 





 
 
 

 
 Digital documentation solutions 
 Did you know that sets are often incomplete or incorrectly packed? Many sets often do not contain what the surgeon needs or even have the wrong instruments inside.  [1] 
 The reprocessing of medical devices is becoming more complex and complicated every day. If documentation is not performed professionally, instruments disappear, transparency regarding entire instrument cycle flow is missing, instrument movement between sets or disciplines cannot be tracked and OR complains because of inappropriate set assembling.

 instacount® 
 Take control of processes at CSSD – OR; generating, collecting and analyzing data 
 instacount ® instrument management system is a computer assisted system that makes it easy for you to manage and to document sterile goods supply. Its modular structure means that  instacount ®   can be individually adapted to requirements and financial possibilities of customers. 
   
 Your possible benefits: 
 Comprehensive documentation of sterile goods supply Optimized and standardized procedures Structured and standardized stock management Cost transparency in the entire reprocessing cycle Collect reports of your entire SGM process in seconds
