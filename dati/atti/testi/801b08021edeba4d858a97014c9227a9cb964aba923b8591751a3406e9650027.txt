Overview Ratings + reviews Details + support MoovA towards a new mobility MoovA is the Almaviva Group's modular, scalable, and integrated platform
that combines a set of state-of-the-art technologies and components in line
with the newest and most modern innovation paradigms in mobility and logistics,
to accompany the development of all Business areas and drive a responsible and
sustainable digital transition process of the sector. 

 Moova is a technologically advanced platform that adopts a distinctive
conception in the transportation sector and enables concrete answers in the
digital transformation paths of companies and mobility ecosystems. 

 The platform offers multiple products within an integrated suite, aimed
at the different operators in the mobility and logistics sectors and the
Authorities that govern the different transport ecosystems. The products
support key core processes, from transportation planning and management,
vehicle and infrastructure maintenance, sales and customer information, and
intermodal logistics management. 

 In addition, Moova's products implement an innovative Transport
Digitalization concept, which enables a truly integrated and intelligent
digital view of the entire transportation system. 

 

 Below
are the main modules of the suite and their capabilities: 

 Information Hub: it provides an
integrated, coherent and detailed digital view of the entire mobility system.
Based on innovative technologies to collect huge amounts of data and
information from different sources, organizing them consistently according to a
common data model based on international standards and market best practices. Smart train and vehicle: it’s a
turnkey solution for in-house system design, software and hardware development,
manufacturing, project management, commissioning, technical support, and
in-house maintenance throughout the product life cycle. NSP: it enables Network and service
planning enables multimodal management of transportation networks, schedules,
and mobility services. Transport management: multi-modal
& Multi-company: it’s dedicated to all transport actors, local and
long-distance, single-modal or multi-modal transport operators. Designed for
different centralized or distributed operating models (e.g., on-board,
in-plant). AFC (automated fare collection):
automated fare collection adapts to all transportation contexts due to its
modular system. It meets the needs of the main stakeholders: transport
operators, consortia of operators or local mobility authorities. PIS (passenger information system): it’s
a unique and specialized environment to inform the passenger. Natively
Multimodal, Multichannel and Multicultural to meet all different adoption
needs. Program configuration for content, channels, formats, rules and timing
of dissemination. Control room: it’s an integrated view
that allows monitoring of the status of the entire mobility system: of service
performance and all elements of operations (rides, stations and stops,
facilities and vehicles, etc.). Smart station: it’s an IOT suite for
terminal hub management, focusing on security, device management, remote
control, video management, power management, security, and more. The service is
effectively managed with IoT and operational intelligence, correlating
information and using advanced algorithms. Audience: 

 Transport Operator Long Haul Operator Mobility Operator Transport Authority
