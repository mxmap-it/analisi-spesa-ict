 Aci Mobility 
 Who we are 
   
 We are a company wholly owned by the Automobile Club of Italy, a national public body, for which we operate in the infomobility sector and related advanced services. 
 
 
 -->

 

 

 
 
 

 
 We specialize in: 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 Mobility Technologies 
 
 Digital solutions for infomobility, geolocalization and innovative services dedicated to those on the move. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Road safety 
 
 Systems and tools to monitor and improve driver safety , in collaboration with organizations and institutions. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Traffic and road data 
 
 Collect, analyze and manage real-time data to optimize circulation. 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Traffic monitoring 
 We collect and analyze data in real time to support traffic management and reduce road congestion. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Services for Institutions and Private Individuals 
 We provide dedicated solutions to Public Administrations, law enforcement and companies for safer and more efficient mobility. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Digital platforms 
 We design technological systems to manage mobility intelligently, integrating data, maps and predictive tools. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Infomobility and security 
 We develop information systems to update on traffic, accidents and road conditions, improving road safety.
