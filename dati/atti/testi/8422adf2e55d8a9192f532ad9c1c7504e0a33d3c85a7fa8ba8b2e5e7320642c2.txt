 Certification 
 
 
 
 
 Certification 
 The Agency, through certification and surveillance activities, evaluates IT products and services and conducts inspections and verification activities with respect to regulatory compliance in the field of cybersecurity. The Agency exercises the functions of National Assessment and Certification Centre (NCCA), called to apply future European certification schemes pursuant to EU Cybersecurity Act (Regulation (EU) 2019/881).
