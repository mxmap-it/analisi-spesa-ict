 PNRR Migration to the National Strategic Hub 

 
 
 
 
 
 



 
 
 



 

 
 




 
 

 
 
 
 

 Department for Digital Transition of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers – Funding €1,604,222.00 
 MISSION 1: Digitalisation, innovation, competitiveness, culture and tourism 
 COMPONENT 1: Digitalisation, innovation and security in Public Administration 
 INVESTMENT 1.1: Cloud PA / National Strategic Hub 
 The User Agreement signed in March 2024 between ISPRA and Polo Strategico Nazionale S.p.A. , the company to which the Department for Digital Transformation – Presidency of the Council of Ministers granted the ten-year concession. The purpose of the project is the migration of certain application platforms and their associated data managed by ISPRA to the cloud infrastructures of the National Strategic Hub (PSN), specifically designed, prepared, and equipped with features suitable to host the migration of data resulting from the rationalisation and consolidation of Data Processing Centers and the related IT systems of Public Administrations. Polo Strategico Nazionale S.p.A. has defined the document “Detailed Migration Plan.” 
 The completion deadline is June 2025.
