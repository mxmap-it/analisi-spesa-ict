 AgID qualification of the openMAINT SaaS service –
 
 22
 July 
 2020 
 
 
 
 
 Tecnoteca has obtained the qualification as SaaS service of the openMAINT application on the AgID Cloud Marketplace portal for Public Administrations. 
 As of April 1, 2019, Public Administrations can acquire only IaaS, PaaS and SaaS services qualified by AgID and published in the Cloud Marketplace. 
 To obtain the qualification of openMAINT , it was necessary to carry out all the steps provided by AgID, with adaptation of its procedures regarding the organizational, safety, performance, interoperability / portability and legislative compliance aspects. 
 The AgID qualified openMAINT SaaS service is now available to all Italian PAs that need an information system for the management of their real estate assets and related maintenance activities.
