 Corporate Learning 
 

 
 

 

 

 
 
 

 

 

 A solution for every training challenge 
 We offer cutting-edge solutions, artificial intelligence systems and gamification paths, making training highly personalised and engaging. Training solutions based on web, mobile and Digital Education environments. Innovative training to support companies in facilitating change, fostering growth and dominating their reference market. 
 

 
 
 
 Training & Business Catalogue 
 
 We create environments and paths to make content engaging and smart, using specific languages and tools. We plan paths that reduce information complexity in favour of corporate knowledge management. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 Digital Solutions Find out more 
 
 Corporate academies and digital platforms are tools for attracting and recruiting personnel, managing their training through the development of technical and managerial know-how and disseminating a unified corporate identity. At Planet we integrate and customise content to support the Digital Transformation of Organisations. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 Financed training Find out more 
 
 Financed training is an opportunity for growth and development not only for companies but also for employees. We support Organisations step by step through the financing procedures for disbursement and final reporting.
