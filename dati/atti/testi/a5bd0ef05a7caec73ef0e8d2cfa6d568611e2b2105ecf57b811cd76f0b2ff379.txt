 Elsynet 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Elsynet is an IT industrial reality that started up in 1981, with the company name Elsy, since September 2007 a company is dedicated mainly to the TLC sector, converging customers and experiences in the new company. The new core business combines IT experience with the new acquired in broadband connections and telephony, for public and private companies. Today Elsynet is a national TLC operator that addresses innovative ICT issues, maintaining its initial IT skills. Elsynet is able to offer transmission solutions, even over long distances, broadband connections via radio and cable, on which to support the most innovative IT services.
