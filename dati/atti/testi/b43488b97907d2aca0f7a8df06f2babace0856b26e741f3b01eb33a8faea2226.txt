 Cloud services and cybersecurity 
 

 Nivola, the cloud for the Public Sector 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 


 
 
 The simple, secure, Italian cloud 

 The platform for digital innovation in the public administration. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 



 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Nivola is our fully open-source platform, certified to provide specific cloud infrastructure and services for the public administration 

 Connecting to the CSI cloud means having access to platforms, applications for administrations and services for citizens and businesses.   
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Why choose Nivola?

 
 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 Autonomy in management 
 
 Clients can choose whether to manage the resources directly or through CSI. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 Total data control 
 
 The data is held in Italy, and CSI provides direct assistance and support. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 A world of services 
 
 Access to a wide range of solutions for municipalities and health authorities. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 The highest levels of security 
 
 CSI is a “C” type Provider, the highest level expected of a cloud manager. Nivola is QI2 and QC2 certified to host ordinary and critical data and services in accordance with the requirements of the Italian national agency for cybersecurity (ACN). 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 Cloud for ICT companies 
 
 Companies can port public administration SaaS applications to Nivola and provide them to clients complete with all infrastructure services. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 

 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 



 
 
 
 
 
 Nivola's features 
 





 
 

 

 

 
 
 
 Modular and interoperable
 
 

 

 
 
 
 Simple and transparent
 
 

 

 
 
 
 Available across the territory
 
 

 

 
 
 
 Reliable, scalable and powerful
 
 

 
 

 

 
 
 
 

 It can be used to migrate and create tailor-made services for public administrations and integrates with digital identity tools and public protocols (Spid, PagoPA) 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 

 No initial investment in infrastructure required. 
Pay-per-use services and timely cost control through the dashboard 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 

 Nivola is a multi-region cloud. 
Users connect to the region closest to them, cutting latency and response times 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 

 5000 Cores of computing power 

 180 Terabytes of RAM for high performance 

 2+ Petabytes of data storage 

 5+ Petabytes of backup for security 

 10.000+ active requests across IaaS, DBaaS, StaaS
